## Ownership — UNRESOLVED, the key item
- Trend Micro Incorporated is **publicly listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange
  (TYO/TSE 4704)**.
- **February 2025 (Reuters exclusive, carried by The Insurer and others):**
  **Bain Capital, Advent International and EQT AB** were reported among private
  equity firms competing to take Trend Micro private; **KKR** also named as an
  interested party. Market value at the time **~¥1.32 trillion (~$8.54B)**.
- Reporting explicitly noted **a deal is not guaranteed** and Trend Micro could
  remain independent. Exploration of a sale reportedly began after inbound
  interest in 2024.
- **As of 2026-08-26 no transaction has been announced.** The wiki page carries
  `ownership_state: public` with `verify_after: 2027-02-26`.

## Product
- **Trend Vision One** is the consolidated platform: attack surface risk
  management (**ASRM**), cross-layer protection across hybrid environments, and
  next-generation **XDR**, with generative AI throughout.
- **Security Operations** (formerly Trend Micro XDR) consolidates internal and
  third-party telemetry with AI/ML and correlated detection models.
- **AI Companion**: guides investigations, suggests next steps, creates
  automations.
- Named a **worldwide Leader in XDR by IDC MarketScape**.
- Founded 1988; HQ Tokyo.
