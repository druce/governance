## Product / positioning
- Core pitch is the **"SASE Firewall"**: firewall, SD-WAN and SASE functionality
  integrated on a single **FortiOS** operating system, powered by purpose-built
  **FortiASIC** silicon. This single-OS convergence is the stated differentiator
  against cloud-only SSE vendors.
- 2026 launches cited in the Q2 release: **FortiGate 1200G with FortiSASE
  Outpost** (targeting high-performance AI infrastructure), the **FortiSOC AI SOC
  platform**, and **expanded FortiEndpoint**.
