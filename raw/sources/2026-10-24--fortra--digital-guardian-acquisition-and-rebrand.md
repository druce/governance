## Extract
- HelpSystems press release dated **2021-10-27**: "HelpSystems announced today the acquisition of Digital Guardian, the industry's only SaaS provider of data loss prevention (DLP) solutions for large and mid-sized organizations." Financial terms not disclosed. Joins HelpSystems data-security lineup with GoAnywhere, Clearswift, Agari, Titus. Release wording is "announced today the acquisition" — i.e. announced as completed; no separate closing release found. IT Jungle (2021-12-01) describes the deal as done in late October 2021. Treat announced = closed = 2021-10-27 (medium-high confidence).
- Digital Guardian: founded 2003 as **Verdasys**, renamed Digital Guardian 2014 (per dope.security article, see companion file). Flagship "DG Data Protection Platform" plus a managed security program (managed DLP).
- HelpSystems → **Fortra**: press release dated **2022-11-02** "HelpSystems announced today that it has become Fortra". HQ Eden Prairie, Minnesota.
- **Brand retirement:** digitalguardian.com 301-redirects to fortra.com/platform/data-loss-prevention (checked 2026-10-24). Fortra's Japanese page is titled "Digital Guardian is now Fortra DLP". Agent/console names still DG Agent / DGMC (per dope.security).
- **Ownership:** HGGC controlling interest 2018-04; TA Associates + Charlesbank Capital Partners investment 2019-10; Harvest Partners SCF minority investment 2021-05. Lincoln International (2025, JAMS divestiture to PSG Equity / 2ndWave) describes Fortra as "a portfolio company of TA Associates, Charlesbank Capital Partners and Harvest Partners" (HGGC not named; HGGC's own portfolio page still lists Fortra as current). Which sponsor controls is unclear. No sale of Fortra found through 2026-10-24.
