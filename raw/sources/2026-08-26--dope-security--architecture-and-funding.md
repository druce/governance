## Architecture (vendor claims — treat as marketing)
- **Fly Direct**: inspection happens **on the endpoint**; traffic goes straight to
  the internet with no "stopover" vendor data centre in the path.
- Lightweight agent inspects TLS **locally**; claimed **under 100MB RAM** and **up
  to 4x** the performance of legacy proxy SWGs.
- **Dopamine DLP** — AI governance described as native rather than an add-on,
  catching sensitive data in **prompts and uploads**.
- Vendor-cited references: a Fortune 100 running the agent on 18,000+ devices;
  Outreach Health securing 99% of its fleet in a week and cutting web-access IT
  tickets 70% in 90 days.

## Funding
- **~$24.0M raised across 3 rounds**; most recent **$16.0M Series A, March 2023**.
- Founded 2021, Palo Alto CA. Founder Kunal Agarwal (ex-Forcepoint).
- No round found since 2023 — the main viability question, given it competes with
  Zscaler, Netskope, Palo Alto and Fortinet.

## Caveat on sourcing
dope.security's blog publishes its own competitive "buyer's guides" and
alternatives pages that rank it favourably. These rank well on category searches
and are **vendor marketing, not analysis**. No independent analyst placement found.

## Confidence
- Architecture/performance claims: low (vendor only, uncorroborated).
- Funding: medium (aggregator).
