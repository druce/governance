- Press release dated **2026-06-29**: SailPoint **completed** its acquisition of Entro Security. Price **not disclosed** in the release.
- Entro: non-human identity (NHI) and credentials security — discovers and secures 1,200+ types of secrets, tokens, and certificates across CI/CD pipelines, codebases, and container registries; monitors token behavior for anomalies; detects malicious AI tool calls via its "NHIDR" technology.
- Integration: Entro's NHI and credentials solutions available **immediately as standalone offerings** to SailPoint customers; native integration into **SailPoint Agentic Fabric** continues (deep secrets discovery + SailPoint identity governance).
- Co-founders **Itzik Alvas** (CEO) and **Adam Cheriki** (CTO) joined SailPoint's technology organization.
- SailPoint CEO Mark McClain quote: "a real-time, unified control plane to govern, secure, and manage the lifecycle of every single identity — human, machine, or AI agent."
