## Seed (TechCrunch 2025-11-17)
- Out of stealth 2025-11-17 with **$11M seed** from Khosla Ventures' Keith Rabois and Felicis.
  Runlayer's own blog title: "Led by Keith Rabois at Khosla Ventures with Felicis".
- Founders: **Andrew (Andy) Berman** (CEO; third-time founder — Nanit; Vowel, acquired by
  Zapier 2024; then Zapier director of AI), **Tal Peretz**, **Vitor Balocco** (both ex-Zapier).
- Product at launch: gateway, threat detection on MCP requests, observability, fine-grained
  permissions integrating with IdPs like **Okta and Entra**, Okta-style catalog of pre-vetted
  MCP servers, agent permissions mapped to each user's permissions.
- "Dozens of customers," 8 unicorns/public cos; named Gusto, dbt Labs, Instacart, Opendoor.
- Advisors/angels: David Soria Parra (MCP co-creator, Anthropic), Travis McPeak (Cursor), Nikita Shamgunov (Neon).

## Series A (Runlayer blog 2026-06-24; Fortune 2026-06-24)
- **$30M Series A**, Felicis and Khosla; **total raised $42M**.
- Fortune: **Felicis led (preempted)**; Felicis GP Jake Storm "led both rounds"; Khosla participated.
  (Soft discrepancy with the seed-era framing that Rabois/Khosla led the seed.)
- Fortune: founded ~10.5 months before June 2026 (≈ Aug 2025). >12 unicorn customers; a
  **Fortune 500 bank** (unnamed) monitors 100,000+ employees' AI activity across 200,000 devices.
  Revenue and valuation undisclosed. Competitors cited: Wiz, Palo Alto Networks, Okta.
- Blog: model-neutral platform for AI enablement, security and control across 5–20 AI clients
  (IDEs, chat, vertical AI apps, agents, Salesforce Agentforce); identity, permissions, policy,
  audit logs, visibility, shadow-AI discovery, runtime risk security. Customers: Instacart, Gusto,
  Decagon, Opendoor, dbt Labs, AngelList, Lemonade, "several Fortune 500 companies".
