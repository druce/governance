## Price — and the SEC-filing caveat

Both the announcement and the completion release say **approximately US$205 million**; the
announcement adds the qualifier "after giving effect to expected purchase price adjustments."
Announcement-era guidance (not repeated in the completion release): LayerX ARR ~$10M at year
end, and ~$0.12 of non-GAAP EPS dilution for FY2026.

**No SEC filing carries an audited figure yet, as of 2026-07-27.** Checked Akamai's EDGAR
submissions index directly:

- No **8-K** was filed for the completion. Akamai filed 8-Ks on 2026-05-13 (items 5.02/5.03/5.07),
  2026-05-18 and 2026-05-22 (credit agreement / notes offering), and 2026-05-20 (item 8.01) —
  none for LayerX. A ~$205M deal is below Akamai's significant-acquisition reporting threshold,
  so Item 2.01 was not triggered.
- The last **10-Q** on file is for Q1 2026 (period ended 2026-03-31, filed 2026-05-08) — before
  the deal was even announced.
- The Q2 2026 10-Q (period ended 2026-06-30) was **not yet filed** on 2026-07-27. Because the
  deal closed 2026-07-02, two days after quarter-end, LayerX can only appear there as a
  *subsequent event*, not as a purchase-price allocation.
- The audited **purchase-price allocation** — goodwill, intangibles, and their useful lives,
  the numbers that in this sweep have repeatedly corrected press-reported prices — should first
  appear in Akamai's **Q3 2026 10-Q**, expected around November 2026. That is the figure to
  re-verify against; treat ~$205M as the acquirer-stated, unaudited number until then.

## Brand status (checked 2026-07-27)

Split, and worth stating precisely because the two halves point in different directions:

- **layerxsecurity.com is live (HTTP 200, no redirect) and still LayerX-branded** — LayerX logo,
  LayerX navigation, product called the "LayerX Interaction Security Platform." The only Akamai
  content is a banner across the top:

  > "Akamai acquires LayerX, delivering end-to-end security and real-time AI usage control"

  with a "Learn More" link to Akamai's newsroom.

- **On akamai.com the product has already been renamed.** The product page at
  akamai.com/products/akamai-workforce-protector is titled **"Akamai Workforce Protector
  (Formerly LayerX)"** and positions it as an "interaction security" layer: a browser
  extension plus management console and cloud intelligence that "control[s] prompts, responses,
  agent actions, and data exchanges across AI, SaaS, web, and desktop applications," covering
  shadow-AI/SaaS discovery, GenAI/web/SaaS data-leakage prevention, and malicious-extension
  protection, on managed and unmanaged devices. A Gartner Peer Insights listing already exists
  under "Akamai Workforce Protector."

So the acquirer has picked a successor name and is using it, while the acquired company's own
domain has not yet been migrated. Expect layerxsecurity.com to fold into akamai.com; the
"formerly LayerX" parenthetical is the standard Akamai transition marker and is not permanent.
