# DeepKeep — footer compliance badges (correction source)

- URL: https://www.deepkeep.ai/about (footer; same footer appears site-wide)
- Fetched: 2026-08-22
- Good for: correcting the claim that DeepKeep publishes **no** certifications.
  It displays four badges. Nothing sits behind them.

## What is actually there

Four compliance badge **images** in the site footer. Alt text, verbatim:

- `ISO 27001 badge`
- `ISO 9001 badge`
- `SOC 2 badge`
- `GDPR badge`

## What is NOT there

- **No Type I / Type II marking on the SOC 2 badge.** A SOC 2 badge with no Type
  is uninformative — Type I is a point-in-time design opinion, Type II is the one
  that tests operating effectiveness over a period.
- **None of the four badges is a hyperlink.** They are static images.
- **No trust centre, no compliance page, no security page, no downloadable
  report** linked anywhere on the page. Footer links are only: Solutions
  (DeepKeep for LLM, DeepKeep for VLM), Capabilities, Company (Partner with Us,
  Media, Careers, Contact, About), and legal — Platform Terms and Conditions,
  Terms of Use, Privacy Policy.
- No certificate number, auditor name, audit period, or scope statement anywhere.
- A targeted web search (2026-08-22) for a DeepKeep trust centre / SOC 2 report /
  ISO 27001 certificate returned nothing about DeepKeep — only generic
  framework-explainer content and other companies' trust centres. **"Not
  retrievable" — this is not evidence the audits do not exist.**

## Why this matters

Badge ≠ report. The correct buyer posture is not "they have no certifications"
(false) and not "they are SOC 2 certified" (unevidenced), but: ask for the
report, the audit period, the auditor, and the SOC 2 **Type**, and treat the
badge as a claim until you hold the document.

where the product page said "DeepKeep for Vision" on 2026-08-22.
