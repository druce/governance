## Key extracted facts

### CrowdStrike Form 10-K, FY2025 (fiscal year ended 2025-01-31), Note 12 "Acquisitions"

Verbatim:

> **Adaptive Shield**
> On November 20, 2024, the Company acquired 100% of the equity interest of A.S. Adaptive
> Shield Ltd. ("Adaptive Shield"), a SaaS-based cybersecurity company that offers customers
> comprehensive SaaS security posture management solutions. The acquisition has been
> accounted for as a business combination. The total consideration transferred consisted of
> $213.7 million in cash, net of $13.7 million of cash acquired, and $0.7 million
> representing the fair value of replacement equity awards attributable to pre-acquisition
> service. The remaining fair value of these replacement awards attributed to
> post-combination service was excluded from the purchase price.

Purchase price allocation (preliminary): identifiable intangibles $31.1M (developed
technology $23.6M / 72 mo; customer relationships $7.5M / 72 mo), net tangible liabilities
acquired $7.7M, goodwill $191.0M. Acquisition costs $2.7M in FY2025 G&A. The acquisition
"did not have a material impact" on consolidated financials, so no pro forma disclosures.

### Same 10-K, Item 5 "Recent Sales of Unregistered Equity Securities"

Verbatim:

> On November 20, 2024, we issued approximately $22.8 million of shares of our Class A
> common stock, subject to service-based vesting and other conditions, to certain
> stockholders of Adaptive Shield in connection with our acquisition of Adaptive Shield.

This vesting stock is post-combination compensation, excluded from the purchase price —
it is most likely the gap between the ~$214M accounting purchase price and the
press-reported "~$300M" headline (which also folded in retention RSU/PSU grants).

### Cash flow statement corroboration

FY2025 investing activities included "business acquisitions, net of cash acquired, of
$310.3 million, which was related to the Flow Security and Adaptive Shield acquisitions"
(Flow Security closed 2024-03-26 for $96.4M net cash; $310.3M − $96.4M = $213.9M ≈ the
$213.7M Adaptive Shield figure).
