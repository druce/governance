# Twilio SEC filings — Stytch acquisition (announced + closed + price)

URL (10-K FY2025): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1447669/000144766926000021/twlo-20251231.htm
URL (10-Q Q3 2025): https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1447669/000144766925000151/twlo-20250930.htm
fetched: 2026-07-27
Good for: PRIMARY (acquirer SEC filings) confirmation of BOTH the announced date and the closed
date of Twilio's acquisition of Stytch, plus the purchase price that Twilio's blog left undisclosed.

## Announced date — Twilio Form 10-Q, Q3 2025 (period ended 2025-09-30, filed 2025-10-31)

Verbatim (MD&A):

> "On October 30, 2025, we entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Stytch, Inc., an identity
> platform for AI agents. We expect the transaction to close in the fourth quarter of 2025, subject
> to customary closing conditions."

Also listed among forward-looking statements: "the timing and completion of our proposed acquisition
of Stytch, Inc." — i.e. as of the 10-Q filing the deal was **announced but not yet closed**.

Corroborating filing: Form 8-K filed 2025-10-30 (items 2.02, 9.01), Q3 2025 earnings release
(accession 0001447669-25-000147, exhibit `twloq325ex991.htm`).

## Closed date + price — Twilio Form 10-K, FY2025 (period ended 2025-12-31)

Verbatim (Note 11, Business Combinations):

> "On November 14, 2025, the Company acquired all outstanding shares of Stytch, Inc. ("Stytch"), an
> identity platform for AI agents based in San Francisco, California, for a purchase price of
> $104.1 million paid in cash, or $58.5 million net of cash acquired. The acquisition was accounted
> for as a business combination and the total preliminary purchase price of $104.1 million was
> allocated to the net tangible and intangible assets and liabilities based on their fair values on
> the acquisition date with the excess recorded as goodwill."

Preliminary purchase price allocation (in thousands):

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash and cash equivalents | $45,565 |
| Other tangible assets and liabilities, net | $(1,844) |
| Intangible assets | $11,833 |
| Goodwill | $48,507 |
| **Total preliminary purchase price** | **$104,061** |
| Less: cash and cash equivalents acquired | $(45,565) |
| **Total preliminary purchase price, net of cash acquired** | **$58,496** |

Identifiable intangibles: developed technology $9,864K (5-yr life); customer relationships $1,969K
(4-yr life). Total $11,833K.

## Analyst note (not from the filing)

$104.1M gross is **below** Stytch's ~$120M in disclosed primary funding ($30M Series A + $90M
Series B) and far below the $1B Series B valuation (2021-11). Only ~$11.8M of the price was
allocated to identifiable intangibles and ~$45.6M of the purchase price was Stytch's own balance-
sheet cash, so the effective enterprise value was ~$58.5M. This reads as a soft landing / acqui-hire
-flavored outcome rather than a premium exit — relevant to roadmap-durability diligence.

## Corroboration on the close (non-SEC, secondary but primary-to-Stytch)

- Twilio blog "Twilio Acquired Stytch, an Identity Platform for AI Agents"
  (https://www.twilio.com/en-us/blog/company/news/twilio-to-acquire-stytch): "On November 14, Twilio
  completed its acquisition of Stytch." Terms not disclosed in the blog.
- Stytch changelog 2025-11-14, "A new chapter begins: Stytch joins Twilio"
  (https://changelog.stytch.com/announcements/2025-11-14-a-new-chapter-begins-stytch-joins-twilio):
  "It's official, Twilio's acquisition of Stytch is now final!" Twilio Inc. becomes parent company
  and data controller; Stytch, Inc. continues as day-to-day data processor.

## Brand status as of 2026-07-27

stytch.com is live (HTTP 200, no redirect) and still Stytch-branded ("The identity platform for
humans & AI agents"), with a banner: "Stytch has joined Twilio to build the intelligent identity
layer for the internet." Brand and product names persist post-close.
