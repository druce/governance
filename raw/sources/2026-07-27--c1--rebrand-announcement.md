# ConductorOne rebrands to C1

- URL: https://www.c1.ai/blog/wearec1 (vendor blog, "We Are C1") + https://www.c1.ai/news/press-release/introducing-ai-access-management
- Fetched: 2026-07-27
- Good for: confirms ConductorOne → C1 rename (announced 2026-04-06), new domain c1.ai, rationale, no ownership change.

## Facts

- **Rebrand**: ConductorOne announced its rename to **C1** on **2026-04-06**
  ("We Are C1" blog post). New website: **c1.ai**.
- Rationale (CEO Alex Bovee): repositioning around "access for the agentic
  era" — "AI agents will far outnumber human users. They will make thousands of
  autonomous decisions per minute." Brand evolution, not a disclosed legal-entity
  change; existing capabilities remain, with added AI visibility, agentic
  identity, AI governance, and a unified identity context graph.
- A 2026-03-19 press release (AI Access Management launch) still used the
  ConductorOne name; C1's own newsroom now titles it "C1 Announces..." — the
  rename landed between mid-March and 2026-04-06.
- No funding or ownership change mentioned in rebrand coverage — still
  independent, VC-backed ($79M Series B Oct 2025, Greycroft-led; see
  2026-06-28 sources on the vendor page).

## Disambiguation hazard

- **"C1" is also the name of ConvergeOne**, a large IT-services MSP that
  rebranded to C1 in October 2023 (onec1.com; announced One C1 strategy
  2023-10-11; filed Chapter 11 April 2024). Two unrelated companies now share
  the C1 brand — survey options and wiki prose should say
  "C1 (formerly ConductorOne)" to avoid confusion.
  Sources: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/convergeone-announces-one-c1-strategy-301953605.html
