## Facts

- **Funding**: $35M Series B announced 2022-06-07, led by **JMI Equity**, with
  Album VC, Emergence Capital, Forgepoint Capital, FJ Labs. Total raised: ~$51M.
  No acquisition or later round found as of 2026-07-27 → `independent`.
- Founded 2015, HQ Provo, Utah, USA.
- Product: vendor security assessment / questionnaire automation, two-sided:
  buyers assess vendors; vendors publish proactive security profiles. The
  "Whistic Vendor Security Network" claims 40,000+ on-demand vendor profiles.
  Current positioning (whistic.com, 2026) is AI-first questionnaire completion
  and assessment ("Whistic AI").
