# "Venminder by Ncontracts" — live site branding check

- URL: https://www.venminder.com/ (own site, HTTP 200, no redirect) + https://www.ncontracts.com/
- Fetched: 2026-07-27
- Good for: confirms Venminder still ships as its own brand/domain ~2 years post-acquisition,
  under a "Venminder by Ncontracts" sub-brand line; also that Ncontracts still markets its own
  separate vendor-risk product (Nvendor).

## Facts (as of 2026-07-27)

- `venminder.com` returns 200 with **no redirect** to ncontracts.com. Page `<title>` and
  `og:title` are still plain **"Third-Party Risk Management and Due Diligence Platform |
  Venminder"**; meta description mentions only Venminder. Logo asset is "Venminder Logo 2025".
- The parent brand appears once, in the body, as a **sub-brand byline**:
  > "Explore the Venminder Platform — No matter your size, **Venminder by Ncontracts** helps you
  > streamline compliance, manage risk, and build trust continuously through a single AI-powered
  > platform."
- Corporate plumbing has moved to the parent: the site's **Careers** links (both mega-menu and
  footer) point to `https://www.ncontracts.com/careers` ("Explore career opportunities and learn
  more about Venminder culture").
- Product names on venminder.com are unchanged: the Venminder software platform, **Vendiligence**
  (control assessments), **Ven-monitor** (continuous monitoring). Marketing claim on site:
  1,200+ customers; 30,000+ risk-rated assessments annually; 400+ banks.
- `ncontracts.com` (checked same day) does **not** mention Venminder on its homepage and continues
  to market its own vendor-risk line, including **Nvendor**, "Vendor Management", "AI-Powered
  Vendor Contract Management", "Vendor Due Diligence".

## Reading

Sub-brand naming convention ("X by Y"), not absorption: separate domain, separate product names,
separate sales motion. But the two overlapping TPRM products (Venminder platform vs Ncontracts
Nvendor) still coexist under one owner ~2 years after close — roadmap-convergence risk for buyers
is unresolved, not settled.

The acquirer press release (2024-09-04) reads "announced today that it **has acquired** Venminder"
