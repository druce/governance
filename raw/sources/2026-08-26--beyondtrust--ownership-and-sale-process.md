## Products
- **Password Safe** — privileged-credential vault, discovery, rotation, session mgmt.
- **Privileged Remote Access** (Bomgar heritage) — brokers vendor/administrator
  access without VPN or shared credentials. The standout capability, and the one most
  relevant to a fund managing third-party admin access.
- **Endpoint Privilege Management** — removes standing local admin.

## Material incident history — raise in diligence
**December 2024:** BeyondTrust disclosed a compromise of **Remote Support SaaS
instances via a stolen API key**, subsequently linked publicly to intrusions at the
**US Treasury Department**. For a vendor whose product *is* privileged remote access
this is material: ask what changed in key management, tenancy isolation and
monitoring since.

## Provenance note
Tangled lineage — Bomgar (Francisco-backed) acquired BeyondTrust in 2018 and took the
BeyondTrust name; earlier lineage runs through Symark and eEye Digital Security.
Founding dates cited vary by which predecessor is counted; treat as approximate.
