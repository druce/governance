## Facts

- **Funding**: $200M Series C announced 2022-12-07, co-led by **ICONIQ Growth**
  and **GGV Capital**, at a **$2B valuation** (double the Nov 2021 Series B
  valuation). Participants: Alkeon, Salesforce Ventures, Cowboy Ventures,
  S Ventures (SentinelOne), SVCI.
- **Independent** as of 2026-07-27: web search for "Drata acquisition" surfaces
  no acquirer. (A rumored SailPoint deal was checked and NOT found — treat as
  false unless a primary source appears.)
- Traction (Sacra estimates): 8,000+ customers, $100M+ ARR by 2025.
- Tuck-in acquisitions by Drata: SafeBase (trust centers, announced 2025-01),
  oak9 and Harmonize (2024).
- Product: compliance automation — continuous control monitoring and automated
  evidence collection across 14+ frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA...).
  Direct competitor set: Vanta, Secureframe, Sprinto.
