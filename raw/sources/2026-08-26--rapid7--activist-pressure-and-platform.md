## Activist campaign — UNRESOLVED
- **June 2024:** **Jana Partners** disclosed a significant position and signalled it
  may urge Rapid7 to **sell itself**, alongside operational and forecasting
  improvements.
- Jana working with **Cannae Holdings**, wanting Cannae to team with a private
  equity firm to buy Rapid7. Cannae later reported to be eyeing participation in a
  deal while quitting the group with the activist.
- **March 2025:** Rapid7 reported **close to a settlement** with Jana — three new
  directors to be added to the eight-member board.
- **As of 2026-08-26 no sale has been announced.** Wiki page carries
  `ownership_state: public` with `verify_after: 2027-02-26`.

## Financials
- FY revenue guidance **$836–842M**. NASDAQ: **RPD**. Founded 2000, Boston. IPO 2015.

## Platform
- **InsightVM** (ex-Nexpose) — vulnerability management, the historical core.
- **InsightIDR** — SIEM/XDR: log collection, detections, UEBA, investigation.
- **Rapid7 MDR** — managed service; how much of the mid-market base consumes it.
- **InsightConnect** — SOAR.
- Stewards **Metasploit** (open source).
- Platform being rebranded to the **Command Platform**; expect several generations
  of product naming in the wild.

## Confidence
- Medium throughout — credible business reporting, but no confirmed transaction
  and the settlement reporting is ~18 months old.
