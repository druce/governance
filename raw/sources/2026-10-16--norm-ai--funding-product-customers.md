## Product
- Citi Ventures (2024-06): LLMs convert regulations into "Regulatory AI Agents" — executable programs that
  interpret scope, make compliance determinations, handle multi-step tasks; humans oversee/validate.
  First agents: rules for **asset managers, broker-dealers and insurance companies**. Founder/CEO John Nay
  (PhD ML Vanderbilt; Stanford CodeX fellow; co-founder Brooklyn Investment Group).
- Company describes a domain-specific language producing human-readable + machine-executable rule representations.
- Prudential write-up (vendor): translates laws, regulations and firm policies into machine-executable logic;
  agents review marketing content line by line and suggest edits.
- **Supervisory AI** (vendor snippet): verification layer evaluating AI outputs (client chatbots, internal
  copilots, agents) against codified regulations (SEC, FINRA, GDPR) and firm policy; rationale + citations;
  independent audit trail; real-time or post-hoc modes. Unverified beyond vendor copy.
- **Norm Law**: affiliated AI-native law firm launched Nov 2025; outcome-based billing; chair Mike Schmidtberger (ex-Sidley) from Jan 2026.
- Legal AGI Lab; July 2026 proposal with Delaware re legal entity type for AI agents.
