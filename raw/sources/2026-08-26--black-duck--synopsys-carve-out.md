# Black Duck Software — Clearlake / Francisco Partners carve-out from Synopsys

## Transaction
- **Announced 2024-05-06.** Synopsys agreed to sell its **Software Integrity Group**
  to **Clearlake Capital Group** and **Francisco Partners**.
- **Closed 2024-10-01.** The newly independent company was established as
  **Black Duck Software, Inc.**
- **Valued at up to $2.1B**, including **up to $475M in cash payable** to Synopsys
  upon Clearlake and Francisco Partners achieving a specified rate of return in
  connection with one or more liquidity transactions (i.e. contingent earn-out).

## Naming history (important — a research hazard)
- Black Duck Software: independent SCA company founded 2002.
- **Synopsys acquired Black Duck in 2017**, folding it into the Software Integrity
  Group alongside **Coverity** (acquired 2014) and Codenomicon.
- At the 2024 close the whole group **took the Black Duck name**, which is now much
  broader than the original Black Duck SCA product. The brand is "inspired by its
  flagship software supply chain solution, Black Duck SCA."
- Consequence: most published comparison content predating Oct 2024 says
  "Synopsys" while describing the same products.

## Confidence
- High. Both sponsor press release and the wire announcement agree on dates,
  structure and valuation.
