## SecurityWeek (2025-10-22)
- Keycard emerged from stealth 2025-10-22 with **$38M total**: seed (>$8M) co-led by
  **Andreessen Horowitz** and **Boldstart Ventures**; **$30M Series A led by Acrew Capital**.
- Other participants: Essence Ventures, Exceptional Capital, Mantis VC, Modern Technical
  Fund, Tapestry Ventures, Vermillion Cliffs Ventures, angels.
- Founded 2025 by former senior leadership from Snyk and Okta; CEO/co-founder Ian Livingstone.
  HQ San Francisco, CA.
- Product (as described): "identity infrastructure for AI agents"; cryptographic proof of
  agent identity/ownership/authorization; contextual runtime access control; dynamic,
  identity-bound, **task-scoped tokens** replacing static secrets/API keys; policy changes
  without code changes; per-agent activity visibility. No customers named.
