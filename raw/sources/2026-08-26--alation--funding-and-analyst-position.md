## Funding
- **$123M Series E**, announced **November 2022**, at a valuation of
  **more than $1.7B**.
- Led by **Thoma Bravo, Sanabil Investments and Costanoa Ventures**, with new
  investor **Databricks Ventures** participating.
- Other investors: Dell Technologies Capital, HPE, Icon Ventures, Queensland
  Investment Corporation, Riverwood Capital, Salesforce Ventures, Sapphire
  Ventures, Union Grove.
- **$340M total raised across 5 rounds.** Prior: $110M Series D (June 2021).
- Founded **2012**; HQ **Redwood City, CA**.
- **No round after 2022** and no acquisition found as of the fetch date. Thoma
  Bravo on the cap table at a $1.7B mark makes an eventual take-private plausible.

## Analyst position
- **Leader, Forrester Wave: Data Governance Solutions, Q1 2025** — Forrester
  highlighted its **agentic AI platform**, **data products marketplace**, and
  collaboration features.
- **2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Data and Analytics Governance Platforms
  elevated AI model governance to a mandatory evaluation criterion** — i.e. the
  category itself now treats governing the data estate as insufficient without
  governing what AI systems do with that data. This is the single most useful
  datapoint here for the wiki's thesis.

## Product scope
- Data cataloging, data governance, data lineage, data quality; policy enforcement
  and workflow automation; data-product delivery and self-service analytics.
- Historical differentiator: mining **query logs** to infer which data is actually
  used and trusted.

## Confidence
- Funding: high (primary — lead investor's own press release).
- Analyst placements and Gartner criterion change: medium (secondary analyst
  commentary; the Gartner MQ itself was not retrieved).
