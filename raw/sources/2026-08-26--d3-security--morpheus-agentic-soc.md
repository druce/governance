## Company
- Founded **2002**, Vancouver BC. Privately held; **no disclosed institutional
  funding rounds found** as of the fetch date.
- Three names in circulation: **D3 Security** (company), **Smart SOAR** (legacy
  SOAR product), **Morpheus** (current agentic platform).

## Morpheus (vendor claims)
- "An agentic SOC layered on a platform built from scratch over two years by a team
  of 60."
- Claims investigation of **up to 95% of alerts in under 2 minutes at L2+ depth**.
- **800+ self-healing integrations** — the largest library of any vendor in this
  category, accumulated over two decades of SOAR work.
- **Unified Intelligence Model**: one purpose-built cybersecurity LLM performing the
  complete autonomous investigation within a **single reasoning context**, producing
  **one unified audit trail per incident**.
- Contrast drawn with SOAR: "SOAR automates predefined steps; Morpheus produces
  investigation conclusions... runs L2 to verdict and drafts the remediation."
- Retains a **built-in execution engine** — investigates *and* acts.

## Deployment
- SaaS, self-hosted and on-prem all offered. **Open question flagged on the wiki
  page:** whether self-hosted runs inference locally or still calls out — decisive
  for the data-control use case, and not answered by any source found.

## Sourcing caveat
D3's site publishes competitive comparison pages naming Splunk SOAR and CrowdStrike
Charlotte AI. All performance figures above are self-reported. No independent
evaluation found.

## Confidence
- Company background: medium. Product claims: low (vendor marketing).
