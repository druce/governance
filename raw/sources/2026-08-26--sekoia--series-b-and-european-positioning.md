  participating and support from existing investors **Bright Pixel Capital**,
  **Omnes Capital** and **Bpifrance** (the French state investment bank).

- Founded **2016**; Paris / Bretagne, France.

## Scale and customers
- Operations in **four European countries**, **100+ employees**, **~50 MSSP partners**.
- Named clients: **EDF, Vinci, SNCF, Mirakl, Marlink, French Ministry of Armed Forces**.
  Note: heavily French, public-sector-weighted — **no financial-services names**.

## Product
- Unified security platform: **XDR** (real-time extended detection and response)
  plus **first-party continuously produced threat intelligence** — Sekoia generates
  its own intelligence rather than licensing it.
- Launched the **Open XDR Platform** in late 2021, a multi-vendor European offering.
- Stated ambition: leader in **AI-native security platform for managed SOCs**.

## Why it matters for this wiki
Every other vendor in `ai-soc-analysts` here is US-owned. For a manager with EU
entities, DORA obligations, or a policy against US CLOUD Act exposure on security
telemetry, Sekoia is the option that exists. **Bpifrance on the cap table reinforces
the sovereignty positioning.** The wiki page flags hosting region, legal entity and
sub-processor list as the decisive diligence items — the whole reason to consider it.
