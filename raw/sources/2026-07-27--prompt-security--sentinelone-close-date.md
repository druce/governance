## Verbatim extract — Form 8-K (filed 2025-09-08), Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities

> **Prompt Security Transaction**
>
> On August 5, 2025, SentinelOne, Inc. (the "Company") entered into an agreement and plan of merger
> (the "Merger Agreement") to acquire Prompt Security, Inc. (the "Prompt Security Transaction"),
> which was completed on September 5, 2025. Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, consideration for the
> Prompt Security Transaction was paid, at the closing of the Prompt Security Transaction, in a
> combination of cash and shares of the Company's Class A common stock (the "Company Common Stock")
> totaling approximately $180 million. A portion of the consideration paid in connection with the
> Prompt Security Transaction consisted of 1,555,099 shares of Company Common Stock. Certain
> portions of the consideration for the acquisition (both cash and shares of Company Common Stock)
> have been held back by the Company to secure the indemnification obligations of certain
> securityholders of Prompt Security, Inc.

## Notes / reconciliation

- **Announced 2025-08-05, closed 2025-09-05.** These are one month apart. The prior wiki value of
  `closed: 2025-08-05` was wrong — on the announcement date the deal was explicitly still pending
  ("expected to close," "subject to … customary closing conditions"). The 8-K and 10-Q both state
  the September 5 completion date independently.
- **Three different price numbers, only two of them primary:**
  - **~$250M** — press reports only (Calcalist/CTech, picked up by Yahoo Finance and trade press).
    Not in any SentinelOne filing or release. Treat as an unconfirmed press estimate.
  - **~$180M** — SentinelOne's own 8-K, "totaling approximately $180 million" cash + Class A stock.
    This is the deal-value figure the company stands behind.
  - **$159.3M** — the 10-Q's ASC 805 fair value of total consideration transferred. Lower than the
    8-K's $180M because purchase accounting excludes amounts that are compensation for
    post-combination service (the 1,555,099 shares are booked at only $17.2M of purchase
    consideration, well below their market value at closing, with the balance treated as
    post-combination stock comp).
  - For wiki purposes the defensible statement is "~$180M per SentinelOne's 8-K ($159.3M booked as
    purchase consideration in the 10-Q); press reports of ~$250M are unconfirmed."
- Prompt had raised only ~$23M (Hetz Ventures, Jump Capital, Okta Ventures, F5) before the deal,
  per CTech — corroborating context, not a primary source.
- Sibling deal for date hygiene: Observo, Inc. was a *separate* transaction — agreement signed
  2025-09-08, completed 2025-09-22, ~$225M per the 8-K / $185.3M per the 10-Q PPA. Don't conflate
  the two Q3 FY2026 acquisitions.
