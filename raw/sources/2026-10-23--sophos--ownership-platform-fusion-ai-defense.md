## SiliconANGLE — 2026-07-15 (Sophos Fusion launch)
- Sophos Fusion rebuilds Sophos Central (625,000 orgs) as one platform across SecOps, endpoint, network, identity, email, cloud; 500+ third-party integrations.
- Rebuilt XDR "runs on the Taegis analytics" — Taegis is the analytics foundation; article doesn't say whether Taegis continues as a separate product name.
- Next-Gen SIEM arriving 2026-08-15, "priced by users and servers rather than by data volume."
- Sophos claims: largest agentic SOC of its kind, 40,000+ customers; AI resolves 52% of cases without human involvement; average alert-to-automated-response 89 seconds (vendor claims).
- Sophos AI Defense: early access August, GA October 2026.

## Sophos press — AI Defense GA (October 2026)
- "finds the AI tools employees and applications are using, including the ones security teams never approved"; real-time guardrails "inspect prompts, uploads, and other AI interactions as they happen".
- Delivered through Sophos Fusion; "connects controls on the device, across the network, at sign-in, and in the browser". AI activity flows to XDR/MDR analysts. No pricing; no MCP/agent-specific enforcement described. Taegis not mentioned.
