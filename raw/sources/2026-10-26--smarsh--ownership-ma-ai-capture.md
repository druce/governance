- Earlier: Quest Software held 60% until 2012 (sold with Quest to Dell); Dell sold stake to Toba Capital 2013 (Wikipedia).
- K1 Investment Management: K1 announced combining Actiance and Smarsh 2017-11-15 (Smarsh/K1 press release "K1 Combines Actiance and Smarsh..."; TechCrunch 2017-11-15). ## M&A by Smarsh
- Digital Reasoning: announced 2020-11-10 (at FINRA AI conference); terms undisclosed; "expected to close in the next 60 days"; no close announcement found. Digital Reasoning's NLP described as powering conduct surveillance at many Tier-1 investment banks.

- CallCabinet (cloud compliance call recording): February 2025 (CFO Dive).
- Others per Wikipedia: Actiance (2017/18 via K1), Entreda (2020), Privva (2021), Digital Safe from Micro Focus (2021/22).

## TeleMessage incident (2025)
- May 2025: TeleMessage (TM SGNL, Signal archiving clone used by then-NSA Mike Waltz) breached; Smarsh suspended all TeleMessage services pending investigation; other Smarsh products stayed online (BleepingComputer). Chat logs/metadata of 60+ government users published via DDoSecrets. CISA added CVE-2025-47729 (2025-05-12) and CVE-2025-48927/48928 to KEV (The Register 2025-07-02).

## AI-interaction capture (vendor claims)
- Microsoft 365 Copilot: Smarsh Capture for Microsoft 365 Copilot announced 2025-06-04 — prompts, files, responses across web, Teams, M365 and chat agents; policies applied. A trade write-up says it uses Microsoft's Interaction Export APIs (single source).
- ChatGPT: Smarsh Copilot channel page lists OpenAI's ChatGPT Compliance API among direct API integrations (search snapshot).
- Claude: 2026-05-21 press release — Smarsh Capture integrates with the Claude Compliance API; captures text, files, Claude-generated artifacts, deleted messages, archived conversations; brought into "capture, supervision, and analytics" alongside 100+ channels; Smarsh says it is among the first certified compliance partners.
- SiliconANGLE 2026-10-22 (Smarsh EDGE 2026, theCUBE paid media partner): Smarsh "launch partner for compliance APIs from Anthropic and OpenAI"; treats AI incl. agent-to-agent comms as a channel; new MCP server; 4–5 agents in six months; compliance language models.
