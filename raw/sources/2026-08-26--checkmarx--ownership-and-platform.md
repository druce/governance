# Checkmarx — ownership (Hellman & Friedman) and Checkmarx One / ASPM platform

## Ownership
- Hellman & Friedman **completed** the acquisition of Checkmarx from **Insight
  Partners**, all cash, valued at **$1.15B**. (H&F portfolio page: hf.com/portfolio/checkmarx/)
- **TPG** partnered with H&F and holds a **minority interest** alongside Insight Partners.
- CEO **Sandeep Johri** since 2023, succeeding founder **Emmanuel Benzaquen**.
- Founded **2006**; HQ **Ramat Gan, Israel**, with US offices incl. **Paramus, NJ**.

## Platform
- **Checkmarx One** is the unified enterprise AppSec platform.
- **Checkmarx ASPM** aggregates and correlates signals from **SAST, SCA, IaC, API,
  DAST, container and supply-chain security** into a single risk-based posture lens.
- Checkmarx integrated **Tromzo**'s reasoning engine and engineering team into
  Checkmarx One; brings Tromzo founders/AI engineers in to power new **Checkmarx
  Assist** agents and "enterprise-grade autonomous AppSec" starting **early 2026**.
