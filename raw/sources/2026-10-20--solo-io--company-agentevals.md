## Company (secondary)
- Founded 2017 by Idit Levine (CEO); Cambridge/Boston, MA. Series C $135M led by Altimeter
  (October 2021) at a $1B valuation; total raised $171.5M. Redpoint and True Ventures participated.
- Searches on 2026-10-20 found no new funding round, acquisition, or layoffs in 2025–2026.
  Dealroom shows an undisclosed 2026 valuation estimate (paywalled) — not used.
- Core business remains Gloo / Istio service mesh and API gateway (kgateway); the agentic
  projects are a newer line.

## agentevals (vendor PR 2026-03-25, KubeCon EU Amsterdam)
- OSS (Apache-2.0) for instrumenting, evaluating and benchmarking agents; OpenTelemetry-based;
  offline (recorded traces) and online evals; trajectory matching, LLM-as-judge, tool coverage;
  golden eval sets that flag regressions; CLI, web UI, MCP server. Levine: "Evaluation is the
  biggest unsolved problem in agentic infrastructure today."
- Same release: Solo.io "is contributing agentregistry to the CNCF" — does not claim acceptance.
- GitHub: agentevals-dev/agentevals, created 2026-02-24, ~166 stars (2026-10-20).
- SD Times 2026-04-17 says agentregistry was "donated to the CNCF as a sandbox project" — premature;
  see 2026-10-20--agentregistry--repo-cncf-docs.md.
