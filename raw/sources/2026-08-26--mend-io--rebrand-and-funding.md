# Mend.io (formerly WhiteSource) — rebrand, funding, current positioning

## Rebrand
- **May 2022:** WhiteSource rebranded as **Mend.io** and launched the Mend
  Application Security Platform, marketed on **automated remediation**
  ("industry-first"). The `.io` is part of the brand.
- Positioning: remediation-first application security across open source and
  custom code.

## Company / funding
- Privately held, **venture-backed**. HQ **Givatayim, Israel**. Founded 2011.
- **~$121M** total funding; key event a **$75M Series D in April 2021**.
- **No round or ownership change found after 2021** as of the fetch date, and no
  acquisition. (Absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.)

## Product direction
- Expanded into **AI security**: risk in AI-generated code, and inventory/risk for
  AI models and AI components pulled into applications.
- Mend maintains **Renovate**, the widely used open-source dependency-update bot —
  the distribution asset behind the remediation story.

## Confidence
- Rebrand: high (primary, vendor blog).
- Funding totals: medium (aggregator data, figures vary slightly by source).
