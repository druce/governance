## Deal type — acquisition (closed), with acqui-hire character

Both sides use unambiguous acquisition language, not partnership language.

- **Helicone (target), 2026-03-03**, past tense on announcement day:
  > "Helicone has been acquired by Mintlify, and our team will be joining them in San Francisco."
- **Mintlify (acquirer), 2026-03-03**:
  > "Today, we're excited to share that Mintlify is acquiring Helicone."
- **Mintlify on X**: "Mintlify has acquired @helicone_ai".
- Third-party trackers list it as a completed acquisition (Crunchbase acquisition profile
  `mintlify-acquires-helicone`; Dealroom news item "Mintlify acquires open source AI
  observability platform Helicone"). Both are bot-blocked (HTTP 403) to direct fetch —
  titles observed via search index only, treated as corroboration, not primary evidence.

**Not a merger** (Helicone is absorbed; Mintlify survives as the sole brand and entity).
**Not a partnership** (no commercial-agreement framing anywhere in either post).
It is a real company acquisition, but with strong **acqui-hire / technology-absorption**
character: the two founders moved to Mintlify, the product was frozen rather than invested in,
and the customer base is being pointed at competitors.

## Dates

- **Announced: 2026-03-03** (both blogs, same day).
- **Closed: no separate closing date was ever disclosed.** Evidence the deal is closed, not
  pending: (a) the target's own post uses completed past tense ("has been acquired") on the
  announcement day; (b) founders Justin Torre and Cole Gottdank are described as joining
  Mintlify in San Francisco; (c) as of 2026-07-27, ~5 months later, Mintlify operates the
  product, decides its roadmap, and is running customer migrations. Typical simultaneous
  sign-and-close for a small private venture-backed startup. Treating the announcement date
  as the effective date.
- **Price/terms: not disclosed** by either party.

## Product & roadmap consequence

- Mintlify: "Helicone will continue operating in maintenance mode—security updates, bug fixes,
  and new models will keep shipping."
- Mintlify: commits to "work closely with every customer to support a smooth migration to
  another platform." — i.e. Mintlify is actively steering Helicone customers *off* Helicone.
- Helicone: "Helicone's services will remain live for the foreseeable future in maintenance
  mode. This means security updates, new models, bug & performance fixes all keep shipping."
- **No hard shutdown or sunset date has been announced** by either party as of 2026-07-27.
- Helicone's technology is being folded into **Mintlify's own AI products** (assistant, agent,
  and workflow products) — "intelligent routing, real-time observability, and seamless
  coordination across models and providers", "integrated routing with multi-provider fallback",
  toward a "unified full stack AI knowledge infrastructure". So the capability survives inside
  Mintlify's docs/knowledge platform; the standalone Helicone product does not get new features.

## Brand status (2026-07-27)

- `helicone.ai` is **live, HTTP 200, no redirect to mintlify.com**. Still self-describes as
  "AI Gateway & LLM Observability", still sells ("Try for free", 7-day free trial, no credit
  card), still lists provider integrations. Pricing page still present.
- Top-of-page banner: "🎉 Helicone Joins Mintlify 🚀" linking to `/blog/joining-mintlify`.
- No maintenance-mode or shutdown notice is shown on the homepage itself — the homepage still
  reads promotional. The maintenance-mode fact lives only in the linked announcement posts.
  Worth noting for buyers: the marketing surface understates the product's frozen status.
- Conclusion: the **Helicone brand persists** as an operating legacy brand under Mintlify,
  not a retiring/renamed brand. No rename is warranted anywhere in the wiki or survey CSV.

## Confidence

**High** on: deal type (acquisition), acquirer (Mintlify), announced date (2026-03-03),
undisclosed terms, maintenance mode, no sunset date, brand persistence — all from both
counterparties' own posts plus a live site check.
**Medium** on: the exact closing date, since none was published; 2026-03-03 is inferred from
past-tense target language plus five months of subsequent Mintlify operational control.
