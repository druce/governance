## Omnix (2025-04-25)
- Credential-security platform: AI-driven phishing-site warnings, credential risk detection on unmanaged apps / shadow IT (checks run locally in browser; logs carry domain + username, not password), risk dashboards, Slack alerts, deployment via GPO/Intune/Jamf, zero-knowledge architecture with threat-intel API. Named customers: Chapters Health System, Cineplex, Crane Co., Port Authority of NY & NJ, Woolpert.
- Microsoft Sentinel integration; KnowBe4 integration; Climb distribution partnership (newsroom headlines, undated).

## Omnix AI Advisor (2026-03-19)
- Natural-language assistant over credential-risk data; on-demand risk/compliance reports; **MCP integration** to connect credential intelligence to external AI platforms; runs in Dashlane's "Confidential AI Engine" (hardware-isolated cloud enclave Dashlane says it cannot access).

## Free plan retirement
- Free plan ended 2025-09-16; free users moved to a temporary Premium trial from 2025-08-05; CSV export window to 2026-09-16 (search snippets).
