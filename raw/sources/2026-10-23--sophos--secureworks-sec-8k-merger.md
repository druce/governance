Note: PRIMARY — SecureWorks Corp. 8-Ks for signing (2024-10-21) and closing (2025-02-03) of the Sophos merger, plus Sophos's completion press release. Best for announced/closed dates and per-share price.

## 8-K filed 2024-10-21, Item 1.01
- "On October 21, 2024, SecureWorks Corp. ... entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger ... with Sophos Inc., a Massachusetts corporation ("Parent") and Project Green Merger Sub, Inc. ... The Buyer Parties are affiliates of investment funds managed by Thoma Bravo, L.P."

## 8-K filed 2025-02-03 (Items 1.02, 2.01, 3.01, 3.03, 5.01, 5.02, 5.03, 8.01, 9.01)
- "filed in connection with the consummation, on February 3, 2025 (the "Closing Date"), of the Merger ... Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent".
- Item 2.01: each share of Class A and Class B common stock "was converted into the right to receive cash in an amount equal to $8.50".
- Form 25-NSE (delisting) filed same day.

## Sophos press release — completion (Feb 2025)
- $8.50/share cash, 28% premium to unaffected 90-day VWAP; transaction valued at approximately $859 million. Sophos backed by Thoma Bravo.
- "Sophos is now the leading pure-play cybersecurity provider of Managed Detection and Response (MDR) services"; >28,000 organizations on Sophos MDR (at the time); >600,000 customers.
- Sophos X-Ops adds Secureworks Counter Threat Unit and its security operations and advisory teams. "In the near term, Sophos and Secureworks are operating business as usual".
- Secondary (search): at signing Dell owned ~83% of Secureworks common stock (Help Net Security / ChannelInsider, 2024-10-22).
