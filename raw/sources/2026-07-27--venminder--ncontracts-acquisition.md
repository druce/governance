# Ncontracts acquires Venminder (via Hg buyout)

- URL: https://www.ncontracts.com/nsight-blog/press-release/ncontracts-acquires-venminder-via-hg-buyout (acquirer press release) + https://www.prweb.com/releases/ncontracts-announces-the-acquisition-of-venminder-via-hg-buyout-302237613.html
- Fetched: 2026-07-27
- Good for: confirms Venminder acquired by Ncontracts, announced 2024-09-04; Hg PE structure; customer counts.

## Facts

- **Acquisition**: Ncontracts announced the acquisition of **Venminder** on
  **2024-09-04**. Financial terms not disclosed.
- Deal structure: **Hg** (the same software PE firm that owns AuditBoard/Optro)
  bought out Venminder's previous shareholders and Ncontracts' investor Gryphon —
  i.e., the combined Ncontracts + Venminder entity is Hg-backed.
- Venminder: unified SaaS platform for third-party risk management (vendor
  lifecycle from onboarding to offboarding), **1,200+ customers**, historically
  strong in banks/credit unions (as is Ncontracts, a fintech-compliance vendor).
- Combined entity serves 5,000+ customers.
