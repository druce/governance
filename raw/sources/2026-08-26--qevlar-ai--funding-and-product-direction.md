## Customers — unusually strong for the stage
- Enterprises: **Mercedes-Benz**, **Sodexo**.
- MSSPs: **Orange Cyberdefense**, **ECI**, **Atos**.
- Reported **10x reduction in investigation time, down to ~3 minutes** (customer-

## Product
- Agentic AI SOC platform: autonomously **enriches, correlates and investigates**
  security signals to a verdict.
- **2026-05-21:** introduced **AI agents unifying SOC and vulnerability operations**,
  on the argument that as exploitation windows collapse, prioritising vulnerabilities
  is the same reasoning problem as prioritising alerts. This is a genuine
  differentiator versus alert-triage-only competitors.
