## Modules (verbatim from deepkeep.ai, 2026-08-22)

1. **AI Firewall** — "Real-time detection & guardrails"; "60+ Runtime guardrails
   for apps & agents".
2. **AI Red Teaming** — "Adaptive evaluation & robustness tests", incl. the newly
   launched "Vibe AI Red Teaming".
3. **AI Lens** — "Visibility & access control across teams" (also marketed as
   AI Discovery and Usage Control).
4. **AI Agent Scanner** — "Run-time protection for agentic systems"; maps
   "tools & reachable actions".
5. **Model Scanning** — "Static & dynamic supply-chain checks"; "MLBOM & CVE
   detection".

Two product lines: **DeepKeep for LLM** and **DeepKeep for Vision** (computer
vision is unusual — most AI-security peers are text-only).

Protects three targets: custom-built AI applications, AI agents (autonomous
workflows), and employees' internal AI usage.

Deployment: single SaaS console, but "SaaS, on-prem, your own VPC or air-gapped".

Logos shown on the site: NTT Data, ST Engineering, EY. Analyst mentions: Gartner,
Omdia. No FS logos shown.

## Founding & funding

- Founded **2021** by **Rony Ohayon** (CEO). HQ **Tel Aviv, Israel**.
- Seed **$10M**, announced **2024-05-01**, led by **Awz Ventures** (SecurityWeek).
- Positioned by SecurityWeek as an "AI-Native TRiSM" (Gartner's Trust, Risk and
  Security Management) platform.
- CTO: **Yossi Altevet** (quoted in the 2026-04-20 Vibe red-teaming release).
- Aggregators (Tracxn/PitchBook/Crunchbase, surfaced via search 2026-08-22) claim
  **~$19M total across 4 rounds** and a **January 2026 round involving OurCrowd**.
  NOT CONFIRMED against a primary source or a press release — DeepKeep has issued
  no funding release since the 2024 seed. Treat total funding as unverified.
