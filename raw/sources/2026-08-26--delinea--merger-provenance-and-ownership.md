## Naming history (READ FIRST — four names, one company)
1. **Thycotic** (product: **Secret Server**) — bought by **TPG** from **Insight Partners**.
2. **Centrify** — bought by **TPG** from **Thoma Bravo**, **March 2021**.
3. Merged **April 2021** as **ThycoticCentrify**.
4. Renamed **Delinea**, **February 2022**.
Any content older than 2022 uses a legacy name for what is now the same vendor.

## Ownership
- **TPG Capital** — majority.
- **Thoma Bravo** and **PSP Investments** (Public Sector Pension Investment Board) —
  minority stakes supporting the combination.
- Five-plus years into the hold on a merged asset: sale, recap or IPO is a reasonable
  expectation.

## Product
- **Secret Server** — privileged-credential vault: discovery, storage, rotation,
  checkout with approval. The Thycotic heritage and the centre of gravity.
- Privileged session management/recording; endpoint privilege management.
- Centrify-heritage identity consolidation.
- Increasingly marketed around **machine/non-human identities** and AI agents.

## Risk to price in
Merged product estates under PE ownership reliably produce **end-of-life decisions on
the losing product line** plus renewal-price increases. Thycotic and Centrify
overlapped substantially — establish which components are strategic before committing.

Also relevant history: Delinea disclosed a **security incident affecting cloud Secret
Server tenants in April 2024** which took portals offline. Directly material for a
vendor holding privileged credentials; raise in diligence.

## Confidence
- Merger/ownership/rename: high (primary vendor releases + trade press).
