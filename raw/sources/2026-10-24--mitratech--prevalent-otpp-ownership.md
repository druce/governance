## Prevalent acquisition
- Mitratech blog dated 2024-10-02: "Prevalent has been acquired by Mitratech, a 35-year global leader in legal, risk management, and HR solutions!" Editor's note: "In October 2024, Mitratech acquired the AI-enabled third-party risk management, Prevalent." Announced as already completed (same-day announce/close). Price not disclosed.
- Dual deal announced 2024-10-02 (Austin, TX) together with Preparis (business continuity / incident management). Sellers per search snippets: Prevalent from Insight Partners; Preparis previously backed by LLR Partners (secondary, confidence medium).
- Post-close: Prevalent leadership said account management and support would not change immediately; roadmap: more AI automation, continuous monitoring, centralized vendor management, tie-in to Mitratech policy/ESG/internal-controls.

## Mitratech ownership
- Release dated 2021-03-24: Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan Board (OTPP) "agreed to a strategic investment"; "Hg, the majority investor since 2017, will retain a minority interest"; TA Associates sells its minority stake (invested 2015). Terms not disclosed; "subject to customary closing conditions." HQ Austin, Texas.
- Secondary (Private Equity Wire, PitchBook, snippets): transaction completed 2021-05-18, enterprise value USD 1.55B; OTPP took a majority/controlling stake.
- Reuters (syndicated, 2025-02-06): OTPP exploring sale of Mitratech at >$4B including debt, Lazard hired, early stage, "not imminent"; ~$230M trailing EBITDA; 20,000+ organizations served. Searches 2026-10-24 found no announced buyer or completed sale.
- Owler snippet: Mitratech received $2B debt financing (item dated 2025-12-06) — not verified against a primary source.
