## Extract
- Announced 2026-02-23. ASKB is a conversational AI interface for the Bloomberg Terminal, in beta, for investment research (mainly company and equity analysis).
- A network of AI agents runs in parallel over Bloomberg data, news, research and analytics; combines multiple commercial and open-weight LLMs; attributes responses to sources; generates BQL for Excel / BQuant Desktop / BQuant Enterprise; ASKB Workflows (reusable, shareable multi-step research templates, e.g. earnings prep); analysis of users' own documents.
- Positioned as added value for Bloomberg Anywhere subscriptions; available in the Bloomberg Professional app on supported devices.
