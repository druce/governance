## Notes (as fetched)

SurePath AI positions itself as an AI governance / access-control layer for
workforce GenAI use — "complete, real-time visibility and control over every AI
action"; "Govern any of the AI solutions that you build, adopt, or buy."

Core capabilities:
- **Visibility** — captures GenAI conversations, audit trails, usage analytics.
- **Risk mitigation** — detects and redacts sensitive data in prompts,
  role-based access controls, policy-driven guardrails.
- **Safe adoption** — manages access to public and private models, branded
  enterprise GenAI portal, enriches requests with organizational data.

Target customer: mid-to-large enterprises (finance, healthcare, professional
services, education named). Deployment: cloud SaaS (admin.surepath.ai console).
Pitch: shift "from blocking to enabling" — find and secure shadow AI already in
use rather than block it.

Site notes the company was **acquired by F5** and is being folded into the
**F5 AI Security Platform**.
