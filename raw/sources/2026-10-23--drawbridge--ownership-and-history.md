## DrawbridgeConnect vendor due diligence (2019–2020 coverage)
- DrawbridgeConnect: platform to manage a firm's cybersecurity program; sends due-diligence questionnaires to third-party vendors, tracks responses/completion, centralizes vendor information.
- Enfusion (Oct 2020) adopted it for vendor due diligence ("efficient and fully auditable process"); Optima Partners used it for questionnaire distribution.
- Whether "DrawbridgeConnect" is still the product name is unverified; current homepage brands the platform "Cyber Risk Intelligence".

## Next-gen cyber risk assessment (Opalesque, release dated 2024-08-22)
- Modules to benchmark and score cyber programs, prioritize remediation, "what-if" analysis, worked through a Drawbridge advisor.
- Benchmark "comparing themselves to over 1,000 other managers in real-time."
- **Crosscheck**: advisors validate DDQ responses against other Drawbridge services (scanning), shared documents, and responses to other DDQs.
- Jason Elmer, Founder and Head of Innovation: "LPs and regulators are requiring more than ever of managers."
