## Extract (egnyte.com/products/governance, fetched 2026-10-23)
- AI-based classification of PII, financial and regulated data; "Sensitive Content Protection" identifies/classifies content across repositories.
- User behaviour analytics: baseline access patterns, flags deviations (unusual downloads/sharing). Role-based permissions; "Data Access Governance" listed as a use case.
- Ransomware: "signature-based scanning and behavioral analytics"; flags encryption / mass-deletion activity; can auto-suspend affected user with customer authorization; snapshot-based recovery.
- Retention, archiving, disposal referenced (no detail).
- Compliance claims listed: CPRA, GDPR, PCI-DSS, SOC 2 Type 2, CMMC, ISO 27001, GxP, SEC Rules 17a-3 and 17a-4; "FedRAMP Moderate Equivalent"; EgnyteGov offering.
- Named integrations on page: Microsoft 365, Google Drive, Amazon S3, Splunk.
- AI: "AI Safeguards" controls which content AI tools can access, with audit reporting. Related products: AI Control Center, AI Assistant, AI Agents, AI Search, AI Workflows, Context Studio. Integrations with ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Agent 365.
