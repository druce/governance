## Product (vendor marketing)
- Homepage: Sevii ADR, "Level 6 Autonomous Defense & Remediation platform" powered by "Cyber Warriors" AI agents; modules: Enterprise/Data Lakes-SIEM (Spike), Identity (Oz), Cloud (Ace), AI Security (Aegis), preemptive (G2, Jäger HK); Cyber Swarm Defense scales agents to threat volume. Automates detection triage, investigation, threat hunting (7-day retrospective, hypothesis hunts), remediation within customer-defined AI governance policies; every action documented; human-in-the-loop optional. "AI SOC" presented as the starting point. 365+ API integrations (CrowdStrike Falcon, Splunk, Tenable, ServiceNow, Jira, Google SecOps, Fortinet, Microsoft); no endpoint software. SOC 2 Type II. Priced per asset, "no AI token or usage costs". Aggregate claims: $107M+ saved, 5M+ detections, 2M+ remediations (no named customers). Investor shown: BMW i Ventures.
- Security Boulevard 2025-09-05: out of stealth; agentless; "mean time to act" metric; remediation under 15 min, often under 6 (vendor claim). Commercial deployments run on AWS; self-contained on-prem appliance with NVIDIA GPUs and no cloud connectivity for classified/regulated environments. Case notes mirror into CrowdStrike NG-SIEM and ServiceNow. Slated for AWS Marketplace and CrowdStrike Marketplace. CEO Curt Aubley; CSO Arnie Shimo.
- Help Net Security 2026-04-28: Cyber Swarm Defense Mode; customer-set remediation SLO; fixed price per asset.
- Carahsoft page: "Selected for the 2026 CrowdStrike, AWS, and NVIDIA Cybersecurity Startup Accelerator" (public-sector reseller listing).
