## Leadership

- Announced 2025-09-08: founder **Edo Liberty moved from CEO to Chief Scientist**.
- **Ash Ashutosh** named CEO. Background: founded Actifio (acquired by Google,
  2020), co-founded AppIQ (acquired by HP, 2005), roles at Google and Greylock.
- Ashutosh has said publicly that an acquisition is *not* the goal for Pinecone.
  Liberty framed the CEO appointment as "signal enough of what we want to do."

## Sale reporting (SECONDARY, UNCONFIRMED)

- Pinecone "reportedly exploring a potential sale," bankers engaged to evaluate
  options — sourced to *The Information* via secondary coverage.
- Speculated valuation "north of $2 billion," against a last round of ~$750M.
- Potential buyers floated in the reporting: **Oracle, IBM, MongoDB, Snowflake**.
- Funding: ~$138M raised across three rounds since 2021; $100M Series B (2023)
  at ~$750M valuation.
- Customer base cited as 5,000+ (finance, media, pharma, AI).
- Status as of 2026-08-24: **no transaction announced.** Treat as a watch item.

## Market context

- Elastic CEO Ashutosh Kulkarni, quoted: *"Vector databases are a feature. They
  are never going to be a business in and of themselves."* Self-interested
  (Elastic sells hybrid search) but consistent with the pgvector/bundling trend.
