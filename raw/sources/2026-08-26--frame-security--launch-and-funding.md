## Launch and funding
- Emerged from stealth **May 2026** with **$50M** — very large for a launch round.
- Led by **Index Ventures**, **Team8** and **Picture Capital**.
- Participating: **Assaf Rappaport** (Wiz CEO) and **Elad Gil** (initially as angel,
  later via **Gil Capital**).
- Team8 is a company-builder foundry — this is a constructed company, not an organic
  startup.

## Founders
- **Tal Shlomo** and **Sharon Shmueli**, both veterans of Israel's **Unit 8200**.

## Positioning
- Building a category it calls **"human risk security"**: defending employees against
  **AI-powered social engineering** — generated, personalised, scaled phishing; voice
  cloning; real-time video impersonation.
- Framing statistic used: **~96%** of organisations run security-awareness training
  yet **~90%** of breaches still involve the human element.
- Plans to expand engineering, frontier cyber/AI research and GTM in the US and
  globally.

## Customers at launch
- **Louis Dreyfus Company** (commodities trading) and **AlphaSense** (financial
  research platform), plus "tens of enterprise customers." Finance-adjacent, which is
  a more relevant reference base than most launch-stage logos.

## Relevance to this wiki's audience
The threat is specific and current for alternative managers: voice-cloned
instructions to an operations team, or a deepfaked video call purporting to be a PM
authorising a wire. Small teams, large transfers, high trust between few people, and
principals whose voices and faces are on public conference recordings.
