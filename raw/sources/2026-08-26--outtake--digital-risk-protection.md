## Research hazard
"Outtake" is an ordinary English word — independent research is unusually hard and
generic-term searches return unrelated results.

## The risk worth flagging
**Autonomous takedown implies automated enforcement action against third parties.**
A false positive means filing an enforcement request against a legitimate domain,
account or listing. Ask what human checkpoint exists before submission, and what the
remedy is when the agent is wrong. Not addressed by any source found.

## Comparison to make
**Doppel** is the established vendor on this wiki for impersonation detection and
takedown — better documented and better funded on public evidence. Outtake's claim
is that the *enforcement* half is autonomous rather than analyst-driven. Since
takedown latency (not detection) is the real bottleneck in this category, that would
matter if true. Test head-to-head on the same impersonation set.
