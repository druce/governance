## Extract (Protecht release, dateline "Sydney, Australia - 21 April 2026")
- Protecht "today announced the acquisition of VISO TRUST", "a US-based third-party risk management platform powered by artificial intelligence (AI)."
- Protecht: "an Australian based global leader in governance, risk and compliance (GRC) solutions"; references a year-earlier "US$280 million investment from PSG Equity". Price not disclosed.
- Plan: "The integration of VISO TRUST's agentic AI capabilities with Protecht's platform" — AI-driven vendor assessments, AI agents for GRC workflows, continuous third/fourth-party monitoring, board-ready analytics.
- Co-founders Paul Valente (Chief Customer Officer) and Russ Sherman (CTO) "will remain with the business in their existing roles."
- Release does not use the word "closed"; wording is of a completed acquisition.
