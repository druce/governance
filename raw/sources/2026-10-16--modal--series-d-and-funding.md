## Series D (vendor blog, 2026-10-14; corroborated by Bloomberg/Axios per Ground News)
- $750M Series D at $15.75B post-money. Led by Accel; Kleiner Perkins joined; existing investors
  Menlo Ventures, General Catalyst, Redpoint, Lux Capital, Amplify Partners, Bain Capital Ventures participated.
- Crossed $1B annualized run rate; **Sandboxes alone ~$300M run rate** (company claim).
- ~3 billion sandboxes spun up to date; scales to 1 million concurrent.
- 200+ employees across New York, San Francisco, Stockholm.
- Sandboxes launched 2023.
- Named customers: Cognition, DoorDash, Ramp, Linear, Hebbia, Runway, Physical Intelligence,
  Generalist AI, 1X, Chai Discovery, Achira, Suno, TypeSafe AI. None described as FS (Ramp = fintech;
  Hebbia = AI research tool sold to finance — neither is an FS buy-side institution).

## Series C (2026-05-21; Reuters via secondary coverage)
- $355M Series C at $4.65B post-money; led by Redpoint Ventures and General Catalyst; Accel, Menlo participated.
- Two tranches: first at $2.5B, second at $4.65B. ARR ~ $60M (Sep 2025) -> ~ $300M (May 2026), per reports.

## Earlier
- Series B $87M at $1.1B post-money (2025, fall).
- Founded 2021 by Erik Bernhardsson (CEO, ex-Spotify, Better.com) and Akshat Bubna (CTO); New York HQ (Sacra).
- Total funding not stated by company; summing reported rounds (B + C + D, plus earlier seed/A) exceeds ~$1.2B — not confirmed.
