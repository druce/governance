## Extract
- HQ Mountain View, California. Founded 2007 (incorporated 2008). Founders Vineet Jain (CEO), Rajesh Ram, Kris Lahiri, Amrit Jassal.
- Funding: seed $1M (2007), $6M (2009), $10M (2011), $16M (2012), $29.5M (2013; Seagate, CenturyLink, Northgate, plus Kleiner Perkins, Google Ventures, Polaris), $75M Series E led by Goldman Sachs (2018); total $137.5M.
- February 2025: GI Partners and TA Associates became majority shareholders.
- Products: cloud content security, compliance and collaboration; ransomware protection; Governance Risk Dashboard; M365/Google Workspace co-editing; vertical software for construction and life sciences. Egnyte Protect + Egnyte Connect merged into one platform in 2020.
- By end of 2019: 16,000+ customers and >$100M ARR.
