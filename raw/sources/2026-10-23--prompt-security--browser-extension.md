## Extracted

- HiBob case study (SentinelOne): extension deployed across Google Chrome, Safari, and Brave; scans prompts in real time and removes sensitive data (internal credentials, financial details, PII) before it reaches external AI tools; field-level redaction (emails, card numbers, internal docs); in-browser nudges; visibility into which AI tools staff use. Quote (HiBob Global CISO Tamir Ronen): "Everything happens in real-time, with no delay and no disruption." No deployment date stated. Edge not named.
- SiliconANGLE (2025-08-05): Prompt "installs a browser extension on employee devices that determines what AI applications are used by a company's workforce"; admins can block a service or allow it while filtering prompts with sensitive data.
- SentinelOne blog: "lightweight agent and browser extensions automatically discover both sanctioned GenAI apps and unsanctioned Shadow AI wherever employees work."
