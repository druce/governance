## Ownership — note the distinction
- **Summit Partners** made a **significant MINORITY growth-equity investment** (2023);
  **Len Ferrington** (Summit MD) joined the board.
- **Insight Partners** was the earlier investor.
- **Not PE-controlled.** Founders **Darren Guccione** and **Craig Lurey** (2011) remain.
- Practical consequence: less of the renewal-pricing pressure that PE-controlled
  competitors carry — but an obvious future control-transaction candidate.

## Growth (investor-published, not audited)
- Since Summit's 2023 investment: **~250 new team members**, **5-year ARR CAGR of 73%**.

## Product
- Zero-knowledge vault underneath everything: encryption/decryption on device, so
  Keeper holds no key. **This is the property to verify, not assume.**
- Layers: password management (humans) → secrets management (apps/CI-CD) →
  **KeeperPAM** (session monitoring/recording, access provisioning, remote connection
  brokering, behavioural analytics).
- **2026:** extended **agentic AI governance** to Endpoint Privilege Manager —
  privilege policy applied to AI agents on endpoints, not only humans. Overlaps
  `non-human-identity`.
