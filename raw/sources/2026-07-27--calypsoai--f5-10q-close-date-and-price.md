### Announced vs. recorded consideration (scope mismatch, not a conflict of fact)

- **Announced 2025-09-11** (F5 press release / 8-K ex-99.1): "$180 million in purchase
  consideration financed primarily with cash." Expected to close in F5's FQ4 FY2025
  (ending 2025-09-30) — which it did.
- **Recorded at close** (10-Q, GAAP): **$145.2 million in cash**.
- The two figures measure different things: the announced headline is total deal
  consideration; the GAAP figure is the purchase price allocated to net assets acquired at
  closing. The ~$35M gap is not explained in the filing — F5 does not break it out. Do not
  speculate; cite both with their basis.
- Widely-cited press ($180M: GeekWire, SDxCentral, Washington Technology, Frontier
  Enterprise, siliconrepublic) all trace to the 2025-09-11 announcement, not to the close.
