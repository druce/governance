## Announcement
- Cisco announced intent to acquire Isovalent on **2023-12-21** (Cisco newsroom + investor relations, per search results). Price not disclosed (Cisco + trade press). Aragon Research: Isovalent had raised ~$69M over two rounds, with Cisco an investor in its Series A (secondary).

## Close (Cisco newsroom, 2024-04-12)
- "Cisco ... announced the completion of the acquisition of Isovalent" — **closed 2024-04-12**.
- "The Isovalent team joins the Cisco Security Business Group."
- Cisco "is committed to nurturing and supporting eBPF, Cilium, Tetragon, and cloud native open source communities."
- "Isovalent's innovative technologies will become a cornerstone of the Cisco Security Cloud vision."
- Price not stated.

## Cisco blog 2026-02-23 (Scott Raynovich, interview with Nico Vibert, Isovalent at Cisco)
- Brands the offering "Cisco Isovalent": "high performance and secure cloud-native networking" across Kubernetes, cloud, data centers and legacy infra. Paired with Cisco Nexus One as underlay.
- Positions Kubernetes as the platform for AI workloads (training, inference, agentic AI); "Cilium is being used by some of the largest AI and large language model (LLM) providers in the world"; "You need to have a secure multi-tenancy platform". Vendor marketing.
- Hypershield / Splunk not mentioned in this post. (Computerwoche, German, quotes Cisco describing Cilium and Tetragon as building blocks of Hypershield — secondary.)
