## Funding
- **$56M Series B, February 2022**, led by **Accomplice**, with **Kleiner Perkins**,
  **Optum Ventures**, **Kaiser Permanente**, **Gradient Ventures**, **Soma Capital**,
  **Gaingels**, **Impatient Ventures** and **Flexport**.
- **$79M total raised.**
- **Michael Viscuso** (founder of Carbon Black) joined the board.
- **No round found after February 2022** — four years, against Vanta and Drata which
  both raised at unicorn valuations and have since made acquisitions. This is the
  central concern: in a three-horse race, the third is an acquisition candidate.

## Company / product
- Founded **2020**, San Francisco.
- Automates evidence collection, continuous monitoring and audit readiness across
  **SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR** and others.
- Positioning per comparison content: **structured workflows and guided onboarding
  tailored for non-technical buyers** — "ease, speed, simplicity." Against Drata
  (engineering-heavy teams, compliance-as-code) and Vanta (startup self-serve).

## Scope note for the wiki
Like Vanta and Drata, this is **certification automation**, not enterprise GRC — not
a risk register or system of record. Filing all of them under `enterprise-grc` is a
taxonomy compromise. With four such vendors now in that category, the wiki page
raises whether "compliance automation" should be split out.

## Sourcing caveat
Much of the comparison content surfaced for this vendor is published by competitors
(Vanta's own "Secureframe alternatives" page, Sprinto's comparison posts). Directional
only.
