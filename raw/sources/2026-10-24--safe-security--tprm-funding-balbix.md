## SAFE TPRM (vendor marketing)
- 2025-05-28 launch: "the industry's first 100% autonomous third-party risk management platform – powered by agentic AI"; "25-plus specialized AI agents automate the entire TPRM lifecycle" (onboarding, due diligence, continuous monitoring, remediation). "Integration with GRC tools and ticketing systems." Free trial offered.
- Product page: outside-in detection of vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, breaches, combined with questionnaire, contract and document data into one score; Inline Review Agent checks answers vs standards; Vendor Interaction Agent chases vendors; Document Analyzer Agent parses SOC reports/policies; Threat Intel Agent (breaches, leaked creds, legal events); Contract Analysis Agent (expiry, SLAs, reassessment triggers); Fourth-Party Discovery Agent (concentration risk); tier-based offboarding. Dollar quantification for vendors not stated on the TPRM page.

- Series C $70M led by Avataar Ventures; Susquehanna Asia VC, NextEquity, Prosperity7, Eight Roads, Sorenson Capital, John Chambers; >$170M total raised (cxodigitalpulse 2025-08-01; CB Insights dates round 2025-07-31). Named customers in coverage: Google, Fidelity, T-Mobile, Chevron, IHG.
