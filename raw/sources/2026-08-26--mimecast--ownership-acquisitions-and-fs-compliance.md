## Ownership
- **Permira acquired Mimecast for $5.8B**, completed **May 2022** (previously
  NASDAQ: **MIME**, IPO 2015). Founded 2003 in London by Peter Bauer and Neil
  Murray.

## Acquisition trail (the HRM platform)
- **Elevate Security** — **January 2024**; per-user risk scoring. Integrated into
  the **Mimecast Engage** platform.
- **Code42 Software, Inc.** — announced **July 2024**; **cloud-native insider
  threat management and data loss prevention** (product: **Incydr**). Code42 was
  itself PE-backed at the time.
- **Aware** — 2024, following Code42; **collaboration security** (Slack, Teams
  and similar). To be embedded in the HRM platform.
- Net effect: Mimecast is now email security + archive + insider-risk DLP +
  collaboration capture, assembled by acquisition over ~18 months. Integration
  debt is a fair assumption.

## Financial-services compliance — the reason this page exists
- **SEC Rule 17a-4:** brokers, dealers and other SEC-regulated entities must retain
  originals and copies of business communications for up to **six years**, stored
  in a format that **cannot be rewritten or erased** (WORM), with duplicate copies
  in separate locations, fully indexed and auditable by the SEC on demand, with
  audit logs documenting access.
- **Mimecast Cloud Archive** is marketed as a scalable, immutable,
  **SEC 17a-4 validated** and tamper-proof archive, simplifying **FINRA WORM**
  compliance.
- **Mimecast Cloud Archive and Supervision are listed on the FINRA Compliance
  Vendor Directory** — a short list of solutions recognised by the regulator.
- Also referenced for GDPR, HIPAA and FINRA supervision via retention policies and
  communication supervision.

## Caveats
- The 17a-4 "validated" claim comes from **vendor marketing**; the underlying
  third-party assessor attestation and its date were **not retrieved**. That
  attestation is what an examiner tests — flagged as an open question.
- Historical: Mimecast disclosed a 2021 supply-chain compromise (SolarWinds-related
  actor; a certificate used for Microsoft 365 connections). Old and remediated, but
  relevant history for a vendor holding a firm's complete communications record.

## Confidence
- Permira deal: medium (trade press). Code42/Aware acquisitions: high (primary
  vendor press releases). 17a-4 / FINRA directory: medium (vendor marketing).
