# Hadrius — Series A announcement + YC company profile

- URL: https://www.hadrius.com/insights/series-a (vendor) + https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hadrius (YC directory) + https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hadrius-raises-27-million-to-build-agentic-compliance-infrastructure-302824196.html (PR, headline read via search)
- Fetched: 2026-08-18
- Good for: funding stack (seed + Series A), investors, founders, HQ, headcount,

## Facts

**Funding.** Announced **2026-07-14**: **$27M combined seed + Series A**, of which the
**Series A is $22M**, **led by CRV**. Participating: **Y Combinator**, **Pathlight
Ventures**, plus angels including founders of **Altruist**, **Jump AI**, and (per
secondary coverage) **FINNY**. The two figures are consistent, not contradictory:
press headlines split between "$22M Series A" (Axios, Dealroom) and "$27M" (PRNewswire,
vendor) because the latter includes the earlier seed.

**Identity.** **YC Winter 2023** batch. Founded **2023**. HQ **New York, NY**
(123 William Street, New York, NY 10038). Team size **80** per YC profile (2026-08-18).

**Founders.** **Thomas Stewart** (founder & CEO; 2x founder, one prior exit),
**Allen Calderwood** (CTO; ex-Google, ex-Chime; co-founded Quantbase), **Som Mohapatra**
(previously founded Quantbase, an investment firm that sold; college dropout).

**Traction claims (vendor, marketing).** "Over 500 firms," ranging from one-person RIAs
to institutions with 100,000+ employees. Named customers: **BBVA, Altruist, Allworth
Financial, World Investment Advisors, M1 Finance**. Two-thirds of customers use 3+
modules. YC profile claims compliance managed for **"over $3 trillion in AUM"** across
the client base. Customer-reported: "up to 20 hours a week" saved, "99% reduction in
false positives," "70% less manual compliance review."

**Product modules (vendor homepage, 2026-08-18).** Six agentic modules:
- **Testing Program** — automated compliance testing + audit-ready reporting
- **Marketing** — AI marketing review and approval workflow
- **People Oversight** — personal trading, attestations, certifications
- **Communications** — email, chat, social supervision with "zero-retention AI"
- **Account Surveillance** — employee/firm trades vs restricted lists and blackout windows
- (plus compliance-manual implementation / branch-location oversight per PR copy)

Also stated on the homepage: **17(a)-4 / WORM retention**, multi-tenant workspaces for
compliance consultants, "policy-as-code" automation, FINRA/SEC-aligned oversight.

**Certifications (vendor-stated, uncorroborated).** ISO 27001, AICPA SOC, GDPR.

- Founders, batch, HQ, headcount: **high** (YC directory is a maintained primary-ish source).
- Traction/efficacy percentages and the $3T AUM figure: **low** — vendor marketing,
