## Company
- Founded Oct 2021 by ex-Google engineers Dan Lorenc (CEO), Matt Moore, Kim Lewandowski, Ville Aikas, Scott Nichols (Contrary; a 2021 article names only Lorenc and Lewandowski). Team built Sigstore and SLSA at Google. HQ Kirkland, WA (third-party profiles). Wolfi = Chainguard's "undistro" base, now Chainguard OS.

## Funding
- Series C Jul 2024 at $1.12B valuation.
- Series D 2025-04-23: $356M at $3.5B valuation, co-led by Kleiner Perkins and IVP; new investors Salesforce Ventures, Datadog Ventures; existing Sequoia, Spark, Amplify, Redpoint, Lightspeed, Mantis. (SecurityWeek says $365M — likely typo; Fortune/GeekWire say $356M.) Total then $612M.
- FY2025 ARR grew 7x to $40M (Fortune); target >$100M ARR by end FY2026.
- 2025-10-23: $280M growth financing from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund (non-dilutive per Reuters/GeekWire; funds S&M). Total raised $892M; $636M in six months.
- No 2026 priced round found. CryptoBriefing (2026-07-16) "raises $800M" headline is cumulative restatement, not a new round.
- No Chainguard acquisitions found (searched 2026-10-16). Competitor note: Echo acquired technology assets from Minimus (third-party commentary).
