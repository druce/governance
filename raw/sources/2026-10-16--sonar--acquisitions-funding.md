## Identity
- Founded 2008 (Olivier Gaudin, Freddy Mallet, Simon Brandhof per Wikipedia; founder lists vary). HQ Geneva, Switzerland (office address Vernier); US presence Austin, TX (press-release dateline). Legal entity SonarSource Sàrl. Brand now "Sonar"; rebrand date not found.

## Funding

## M&A (Sonar as acquirer)
- Tidelift: definitive agreement announced 2024-12-17; terms undisclosed. Close date not found. By 2025-03-11 Sonar described SonarQube Advanced Security (SCA + advanced SAST) as "the first step in integrating Sonar's recent acquisition of Tidelift" — integration underway, implying close by then. Advanced Security GA May 2025 (Server Enterprise), extended to SonarQube Cloud (blog 2025-05-29). SCA: CVEs in direct/transitive deps, license compliance, SBOM.
- AutoCodeRover (NUS spinoff, autonomous program-repair agent, strong SWE-bench results): announced 2025-02-19; terms undisclosed; Singapore R&D team led by Dr Ridwan Shariffdeen.
- Gitar (AI-native code review; fixes PRs/CI failures in GitHub/GitLab): announced 2026-05-21; terms undisclosed; founders Ali-Reza Adl-Tabatabai and Gautam Korlam join; Gitar remains standalone and is sold with SonarQube. Per BankInfoSecurity: Gitar founded 2023, 9 employees, $9M seed from Venrock.
