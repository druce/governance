## Funding
- **$25M Series A led by Menlo Ventures**, announced **2025-03-06**.
  **Tim Tully** (Menlo; formerly Splunk CTO) joined the board.
- Preceded by a **$5M seed** co-led by **Tola Capital** and **Firestreak Ventures**,
  with **S3 Ventures** and **AV8 Ventures**.
- **$30M total** across two rounds.

## Founders
- **Monzy Merza** (CEO) — previously Splunk, Databricks, Sandia National Laboratory.
- **David Dorsey** (CTO).
- Based Albuquerque, NM.

## Product framing
- A **"knowledge engine"**: the system's accumulated model of the environment and of
  how *this* SOC investigates, rather than vendor-authored playbooks.
- Investigates the alert stream autonomously to a verdict with a reasoning trail.
- Early customers described as "some of the world's largest organizations" resolving
  thousands of daily alerts and freeing hundreds of analyst hours — **company-supplied,
  no names**.

## Confidence
- Funding/founders: high (primary release).
- Product efficacy: unverified — no independent evaluation found, which is true of
  the whole ai-soc-analysts category.
