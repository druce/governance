  CEO Nikunj Bajaj: "shadow army of AI agents". Named customers: NVIDIA, Innovaccer, Siemens Healthineers.

- Product page: per-agent/workflow/environment token or cost quotas; RBAC over who can deploy/run/change agents;
  dev/staging/prod separation; MCP tool calls with OAuth2, RBAC, metadata-based policies, all logged; guardrails
  (tool allowlists per agent, PII filtering, restricted actions, custom compliance rules); "Centralize authentication,
  identity, and service account management for agents at the Gateway layer"; VPC/on-prem/air-gapped/multi-cloud;
  "No data leaves your domain." A2A not mentioned on the product page (a TF blog post reportedly mentions MCP and A2A —
  not fetched). Badges: SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR (no report or auditor shown).
