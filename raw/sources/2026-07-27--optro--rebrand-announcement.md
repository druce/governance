# AuditBoard rebrands to Optro

- URL: https://optro.ai/blog/auditboard-is-now-optro (vendor blog) + https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/meet-optro-auditboard-unveils-new-identity-as-ai-transforms-grc-302707325.html (press release) + https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/auditboard-now-styles-itself-optro (independent coverage)
- Fetched: 2026-07-27
- Good for: confirms AuditBoard → Optro rename (announced 2026-03-09), new domain optro.ai, CEO, FairNow acquisition.

## Facts

- **Rebrand**: AuditBoard announced its rename to **Optro** on 2026-03-09, at the
  IIA's Great Audit Minds conference in Las Vegas. New website: **optro.ai**.
- Positioning: "the #1 GRC Platform" — AI-powered platform spanning audit, risk,
  infosec, and compliance; pitch is a shift from "systems of record" to "agentic
  systems of action." Products listed: Controls Management, Autonomous Testing,
  AI Governance, Business Continuity Management, Risk Management, Third-Party
  Risk Management.
- Second rename: the company began as SOXHUB, became AuditBoard in November 2017
  (per Accounting Today / Corporate Compliance Insights coverage of the rebrand).
- **CEO**: Raul Villar Jr. (formerly CEO of Paycor; named CEO July 2025 per
  rebrand press coverage). CFO Hugo Doetsch; CMO April Crichlow.
- **FairNow acquisition**: Optro (then AuditBoard) acquired **FairNow**, a
  "purpose-built AI governance solution," in fall 2025 (~6 months before the
  March 2026 rebrand announcement, per Accounting Today).
- Claimed traction: "more than 50% of the Fortune 500" use the platform.
- Ownership unchanged: no new ownership disclosed in rebrand coverage — still
  Hg (PE), which acquired AuditBoard for >$3B in 2024 (see
  2026-06-28 sources on the vendor page).
