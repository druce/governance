Key points:
- Thoma Bravo completed its acquisition of Proofpoint on 2021-08-31, in an all-cash transaction valued at ~$12.3B.
- The deal was first announced on 2021-04-26; stockholders approved it on 2021-07-23.
- Proofpoint stockholders received $176.00 per share in cash, and the stock was delisted from Nasdaq.
