## Facts

- **Series C: $60M, announced 2025-05-20**, led by **Sixth Street Growth**, with
  participation from **Riverside Acceleration Capital** and **AlbionVC**.
  TechCrunch reports total raised "just over $125M." Valuation **not disclosed**.
- Prior rounds: **Series B $30M, September 2022**, led by Riverside Acceleration
  Capital, with Kreos Capital and Series A investors AlbionVC and Oxx.
  **Series A ~$11M** (2021; UKTN reported a £8M investment led by UK VCs, 2021-07).
- **Founded 2015.** Founders: **Rory Blundell** (CEO), **David Brassely** (CTO),
  **Nicolas Géraud**, **Titouan Compiegne**, **Azize Elamrani**. Originated in
  **Lille, France** as an open-source project.
- **Offices (company page, 2026)**: Denver, Colorado (1615 Platte St);
  London, England (20 Little Britain); Lille, France (143 rue d'Athènes).
  Gravitee presents itself as US-HQ'd (Denver) with the engineering origin in Lille.
  Exact registered HQ not stated on the page — treat "Denver + London + Lille" as
  the verified fact.
- **Ownership state**: independent, private, VC-backed. **No acquisition** found
  as of 2026-07-27; not public.
- **Gartner**: Gravitee claims "Recognized as a Leader" in the **Gartner Magic
  Quadrant for API Management (2025)**. Vendor claim; the MQ report itself was not
  fetched. Note this is the *API Management* MQ, not an AI-gateway ranking.
- Customer case studies named in the Series C post: Sedex, Posten Bring, Michelin,
  Swiss Post. Financial-services case study: **Tide** (UK business banking /
  open banking), live in ~2 months from a Spring-2020 project start.
