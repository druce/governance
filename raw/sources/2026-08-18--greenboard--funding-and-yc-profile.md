# Greenboard — $20M funding / GreenboardGo launch + YC company profile

- URL: https://www.greenboard.com/blog/greenboard-go-launch (vendor blog, mirrors the BusinessWire release of 2026-05-12) + https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greenboard (YC directory) + https://www.greenboard.com/ and /solutions (vendor)
- Fetched: 2026-08-18
- Good for: funding stack, investors, founders, HQ, headcount, product-module list,
  traction claims, what GreenboardGo actually is.

## Facts

**Funding.** Announced **2026-05-12**: **$20M total raised**, including a **previously
unannounced $15.5M Series A led by Base10 Partners**. Other investors named by the
vendor: **Y Combinator, General Catalyst, Commerce Ventures, Transpose Platform,
Liquid2 Ventures, Kulveer Taggar**, and unnamed "strategic industry investors."
Secondary coverage (sci-tech-today, startupintros) additionally names **Wayfinder
Ventures** — not in the vendor's own list; treat as unconfirmed.

**Identity.** **YC Winter 2024** batch. Founded **2023**. HQ **New York City, NY** per
the YC directory. Team size **26** per YC profile (2026-08-18).

> Soft contradiction: Ventureburn's coverage describes Greenboard as Austin,
> Texas-based. YC's maintained directory and the vendor's own materials say NYC.
> Going with NYC; the Austin claim looks like aggregator error. Non-blocking.

**Founders.** **Dave Feldman** (co-founder & CEO; previously led Product at **Guideline**
building financial recordkeeping systems; earlier led Hive AI's JV with Bain & Company;
BS ChemE + MS CS, Johns Hopkins). **Edward "Ed" Schembor** (co-founder & CTO; ex-Amazon
— led Product for search navigation, founding Amazon Fashion catalog team, worked on
recommendations and the team that became AWS SageMaker; BS CS, Johns Hopkins).

**Product modules (vendor site, 2026-08-18).** Platform core plus: **Communications
Archive** (archiving + supervision), **Employee Compliance**, **Marketing Compliance**,
**Firm Compliance**, **Third Party Compliance**, and **GreenboardGo**.

**Communications channels supported (vendor product page).** Email; **Slack & Teams**;
**SMS and iMessage**; marketing platforms; **Instant Bloomberg**. Archive is stated
**17a-4 and WORM compliant**, with search/retrieval for forensic use. **No AI-assistant
channel (ChatGPT / Claude / Copilot) is listed** as of 2026-08-18.

**GreenboardGo.** A conversational, AI-native layer sitting on the firm's compliance
books and records: answers employee compliance questions with policy-grounded responses,
routes complex decisions to compliance staff, chases attestations automatically, and
preps tasks for human review/approval. Positioned as "AI-native automation built into
every compliance workflow," explicitly *not* "a chatbot layered on top of old tools."

**Traction claims (vendor, marketing).** **500+ financial institutions**; protecting
**"over $200B in assets"**; **99%+ customer retention**; **88% of customers eliminating
multiple tools**; **99.14% reduction** in communications-monitoring false positives;
**7-second average** marketing-review turnaround.

**Certifications (vendor-stated, uncorroborated).** SOC 2 Type 2; WORM / 17a-4
compliant architecture.

**Channel.** Listed on **Schwab Advisor Services**' provider-solutions directory
(advisorservices.schwab.com) — an RIA-custodian distribution channel.

## Confidence

- Funding, lead investor, date: **high** (vendor release; consistent across secondary coverage).
- Founders, batch, HQ, headcount: **high** (YC directory).
- Wayfinder Ventures as investor: **low** (secondary only, absent from vendor list).
- Traction/efficacy percentages, $200B AUM: **low** — vendor marketing, no independent
  corroboration. Note both Greenboard and Hadrius claim "500+ financial institutions";
  the counts are self-reported and the denominators are not comparable.
